About

How I work, and what I care about.

At my core, I love making things and bringing ideas to life.

I do my best work where creativity, strategy, and collaboration meet. Over 8+ years in telecom, fintech, and commerce, I've learned that the right direction rarely arrives fully formed. It comes from connecting people, perspectives, and ideas, and from making something early enough that everyone can react to it.

I've chosen to grow as an individual contributor, and I lead through the work itself. I move projects forward quickly, keep design, product, and partners aligned along the way, and lean on testing to show what's working, then iterate from there.

Understand the bigger picture
The customer, the business, the technology, and the people behind the work. The best solutions come from connecting them.
Learn by making
I explore ideas through design, using the making itself to uncover opportunities and ask better questions.
Build on ideas
Creativity is collaborative. I build on different perspectives to find what elevates the work.

I look forward to the impact we'll create together.
